PRIVACY & CONTRIBUTIONS

Trust matters here.

We keep the initial service intentionally simple and collect only what is necessary to review questions or reply to class enquiries.

Question submissions

We collect the question, optional photo, proposed answer, source details and any optional name or email you provide. Submissions are held privately until reviewed. We may reject them, and we publish no contributor email addresses. Please do not upload personal student data or copyrighted content without permission.

Course enquiries

We collect contact details and course preferences solely to respond to the request. A parent or guardian should enquire on behalf of children under 18. No payment information is collected on this website.

Content quality and copyright

Community approval does not make a question an official previous-year exam question. We assign an official label only after checking the cited exam source. Contributors should upload only material they are authorised to share. For rights concerns, contact the site owner through the course enquiry channel once that service is active.

Data storage and retention

When the site backend is activated, submitted information is stored in its configured Cloudflare D1 database. Approved questions may be made public without submitter contact details. The owner should regularly review and delete unnecessary or rejected personal data; a specific retention schedule has not yet been set.

Security

Administrative review requires private credentials. Public pages use encrypted HTTPS when deployed through Cloudflare Pages. No website can promise perfect security. Do not submit passwords, identity numbers, private exam IDs or payment card information.

Before the site launches

The operator must supply a working contact method, review applicable Indian privacy obligations and school/child-protection rules, and finalise a retention policy. This page is a starter notice, not legal advice.
