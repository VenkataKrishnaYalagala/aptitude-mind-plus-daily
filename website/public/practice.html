MICRO LESSON / 01

Units digit.
Big powers.
Small patterns.

Why do powers of 7 end in the same four digits, again and again?

NUMBER SYSTEMS

Look at the repeating pattern.

Only the last digit matters. Repeatedly multiplying by 7 gives these units digits:

7¹7
7²9
7³3
7⁴1

The pattern then repeats: 7, 9, 3, 1. To find the units digit of 72026, divide the exponent by 4. Remainder 2 means the second digit in the cycle: 9.

REMEMBERWhen a power cycle has length 4, remainder 0 means choose the 4th digit—not the first.
